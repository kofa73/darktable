# Offline opposed brightness harness

`ring.py` reuses the darker-frame loading, Bayer checks, downsampling, and synthetic clipping from `predict.py`. It evaluates six opposed variants and the RATIO-REF reference predictor on one-, two-, and fully clipped quads, recording per-pair bias, absolute error, angle, class counts, and predictor details in `ring.json`; `ring.md` has overall and scene-kind summaries. RATIO-REF is reference-only, as requested.

## Check against `predict.py`

The predecessor `predict.json` was absent. I directly ran `predict.py`’s OPP and metric helpers on the three smoke pairs. `ring.py` matched both `log2_abs_mean` and angle exactly (0.0 difference) for g294 corridor wall lamp, g065 sunset field, and t00 galleria glass roof.

## Run and limits

Processed 103/103 Bayer pairs with no errors; downsampled 88. The single-process, single-thread run took 287.1 seconds wall time (286.6 seconds summed pair time), under `nice -n 19`.

The local radius uses Euclidean distance ≤3 quads. RATIO-REF floors log inputs at 1e-12 because the requested log-space formula has no zero policy. Results are from synthetic clipping of 2×2 quad means; they do not test rendered reconstruction.
